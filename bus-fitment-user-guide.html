🚌 Bus Parts Fitment Diagram — User Guide
Designed by First Bus Franchise · Engineering

About this tool

The Bus Parts Fitment Diagram lets you explore which parts have been issued to your fleet, organised by zone on a visual bus diagram. The data is pre-loaded — just open the file in any browser. No login, no installation, and no internet connection required.

1
Choose your view — Bus Model and Fleet Number

At the top of the page you will see two dropdowns:

  • Bus Model — select a bus type (e.g. Volvo B8RLE). This shows combined parts data across every bus of that model.
  • Fleet Number — once a model is selected, this dropdown lists every individual bus of that type. Pick a fleet number to narrow the view to that one bus only.

The zone badges on the diagram update automatically whenever you change either dropdown.

Tip: Start with a model to see the full picture, then drill into a fleet number to investigate a specific bus.
2
Explore the zone diagram

The bus diagram is divided into colour-coded zones. Each zone represents a group of related parts:

Engine & Drivetrain
Cooling System
Brakes, Wheels & Suspension
Doors & Body
Electrical, HVAC & Interior
General / Unclassified
  • Hover over a zone to highlight it.
  • Click a zone to select it — the parts list panel will appear on the right.
  • Each zone badge shows two numbers: the count of distinct parts and the total number of issues.
  • The General / Unclassified button below the diagram covers parts that did not match any specific zone keyword — click it the same way.
3
Browse the parts list

After clicking a zone, the parts panel on the right shows every part issued in that zone for your selected model or fleet number.

  • Part No. — the material or part number
  • Description — the part name from the transaction data
  • Group — the material group category
  • Issues — the total quantity issued over the data period

Sorting: Click the Issues column header to toggle between highest-first and lowest-first.

Searching: Type in the search box above the table to filter by part number or description — results update as you type. Clear the box to show all parts again.

4
Check stock locations

Click any row in the parts list to open the Stock Location panel for that part.

  • The panel shows every depot holding that part, with the Bin location and Quantity on hand.
  • If a part is stocked at multiple depots, each depot appears on its own row.

Closing the panel: click the × button in the top-right corner, click anywhere outside the panel, or press Esc.

Note: If the stock panel says "No stock records found", the part exists in the transaction data but was not found in the stock file. Contact stores to confirm current bin location.
5
Export a parts list

When a zone is selected and has data, an Export CSV button appears in the top-right corner of the parts panel.

  • Click it to download the current list as a spreadsheet-compatible CSV file.
  • The export includes exactly the rows shown on screen — if you have typed a search term, only the matching rows are exported.
  • The filename includes the model or fleet number and the zone name, e.g. fitment-Volvo-B8RLE-brakes.csv.
Tip: To export all parts for a zone, clear the search box first, then click Export CSV.
